## Single passage: equipment inspection report

**Seabrook Hotel — kitchen equipment inspection**

Inspector: Nina Park · Inspection date: 18 April

Rating scale: 1 = unsafe; 2 = repair soon; 3 = serviceable with monitoring; 4 = good. N/A means the equipment was not fitted at this site.

| Equipment | Rating | Inspector's note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk-in refrigerator | 4 | Temperature controls responded normally. |
| Dishwasher | 2 | A worn door seal causes occasional leakage. |
| Ventilation fan | 3 | Vibration is within limits; recheck next month. |
| Gas oven | N/A | The kitchen uses electric ovens only. |

The maintenance team should submit a repair estimate for the lowest-rated installed item by 25 April. Nina will review the estimate before any work is scheduled.

1. Which equipment should the maintenance team include in its estimate?
   A. The walk-in refrigerator. B. The dishwasher. C. The ventilation fan. D. The gas oven.
   **Answer: B.** 洗碗機評為 2，是已安裝設備中的最低分；冰箱為 4、風扇為 3，氣體烤箱未設置。

2. What can be inferred about the hotel kitchen?
   A. It has no refrigeration. B. Its ventilation fan is unsafe. C. It does not have a gas oven. D. Repairs have already been scheduled.
   **Answer: C.** N/A 的定義及備註顯示飯店使用電烤箱、沒有氣體烤箱。A 與表格矛盾；B 的評分 3 是可使用但需監測；D 要等估價審核後才排程。

3. What will Nina probably do next?
   A. Review a repair estimate. B. Replace the door seal herself. C. Close the kitchen on 25 April. D. Order a gas oven.
   **Answer: A.** 末段說明 Nina 將審核維修估價；B、C、D 都沒有根據。
