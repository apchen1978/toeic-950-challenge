## Part 2: 15 indirect-response questions

GXP201–GXP215 each originally included both a plausible indirect answer and a plainly correct direct answer, making all 15 ambiguous. Both distractors were rewritten to be unrelated to the request; the original answer positions were retained to avoid reinterpreting saved answers. This leaves all 15 keyed to A, a predictable pattern that should be addressed only alongside a compatible history migration. GXP215's indirect response now explicitly indicates that the supplier's reply has not been seen. All 15 question-and-options recordings were regenerated with the existing assigned accents, question/response voices and -8% rate. Similar-sound distraction was reviewed but is not forced into every item; GXP210 retains copy/copier as a sound-related distractor.

## Tips: 8 sections, 16 tips

The official sample and Taiwan rules support listening format, passage counts, no writing on the booklet or additional marks on the answer sheet, and the 75-minute reading section. The three-finger idea remains a personal memory suggestion and is explicitly not a score guarantee. The following claims were corrected: an unsupported 'most common' claim in Part 1; asserted Part 3 answer order; an over-specific Part 4 graphic rule; Part 5's instruction to mark a question number; Part 2 reform attribution; and pace arithmetic (the 10/8/55 example leaves 2 minutes).
