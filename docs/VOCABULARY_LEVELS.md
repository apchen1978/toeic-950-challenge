# 字彙分級 / Vocabulary levels

每個詞條都有一個等級：`800` 或 `900`。這不是官方分數對照，而是幫學習者挑適合自己的起點。

`800` 是核心職場英語，大約對應多益 730–860，也適合以 600–850 為目標的人。標準是：公司信、會議、出差、訂單、帳單、客服裡很常出現，意思通常就一個，不需要法律或財金背景。

`900` 是進階，大約對應 860–990，也就是金色證書那一層。標準是：較少見、較正式、偏法律或財務，或是閱讀題裡容易混淆的搭配詞。原本標成高分、高階的類別，若其中某個詞其實很日常，仍會標成 800。例如 `deadline`、`invoice`、`agenda` 是 800；`stipulate`、`accrue`、`in lieu of` 是 900。

新加入的約三百個 800 詞，主題是辦公室、會議、雇用、訂單、出差、付款、銷售、設施、電話客服、工廠現場。例句是本站自寫的短句，沒有抄字書。既有詞條的英文、中文與例句沒有改寫，只加上等級。新詞另有詞性（`n.`、`v.`、`adj.`、`adv.`、`phr.`、`prep.`）。

Each entry is tagged `800` or `900`. The tag is a study guide, not an official score conversion.

`800` means core workplace English for roughly the 730–860 band, approachable for learners aiming at 600–850. These are words that show up in ordinary office mail, meetings, travel, orders, bills, and customer service, usually with one common meaning.

`900` means advanced language for roughly 860–990, the gold-certificate tier. These are less common, more formal, legal or financial, or easy to confuse in reading. A word that already sat in a high-score category is still tagged `800` when it is everyday language. `deadline`, `invoice`, and `agenda` are 800. `stipulate`, `accrue`, and `in lieu of` are 900.

The new 800 entries are original short examples, not text copied from a vocabulary book. Existing wording was kept; only the level was added. The page opens on 800 so the list does not start as a wall of advanced words. The heading still shows both counts, and 900 keeps the gold-certificate label.
